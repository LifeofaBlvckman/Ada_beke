Our story

Chic, relaxed, elegance. Fashion pieces focused on free size culture: a feel good fashion brand, made in Nigeria.

Who we are

ADA BEKE is a feel good fashion brand, built on free size culture. Getting dressed should feel easy: generous shapes, soft fabrics and pieces that flatter without fuss.

We’re back

Ready for a new chapter

After a little over a year away, we’re finally ready to return to what we love most — bringing fashion, style, and elegance to you.

Shortly after taking the exciting step of rebranding, life took an unexpected turn. The space we were operating out of was sold and we had to evacuate the premises; then the daunting task of looking for a new production space began.

What was meant to be a fresh start became an unforeseen pause — one that gave us time to reflect, regroup, and prepare for everything ahead.

It hasn’t been the easiest journey, but through it all, God has been faithful and his mercy has been evident every step of the way.

To everyone who continued to support us, check in on us, and patiently await our return, thank you for standing by us, even in our silence. Your love and loyalty mean more than words can express.

We’re back with renewed passion, fresh ideas, and an even stronger commitment to serving you better than ever.

This is more than a comeback; it’s the beginning of a beautiful new chapter, and we can’t wait to share it with you.

“Through it all, God has been faithful.”
