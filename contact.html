Size guide

Free size,
explained.

Our pieces are cut generously, so one size is designed to flow on a wide range of bodies. Length and drape differ from piece to piece, so tell us a little about you and we’ll tell you how a piece will sit.

Questions

Good to
know.

What does free size mean?

Each piece is cut wide and flowing rather than to a fixed dress size, so one size is designed to fit a wide range of bodies. If you’re unsure about length, see the size guide or message us.

Do you deliver outside Nigeria?

Yes, we deliver worldwide. Send us your city and country with your order and we’ll confirm delivery for you.

How do I order and pay?

Add pieces to your bag and choose “Checkout on WhatsApp”, or message us directly. We confirm your total and how to pay with you on WhatsApp.

What comes with each piece?

Every listing says what’s included. For example, the Omalicha set is sold with the scarf only, and the Wifey boubou is sold with the co-ord set.

Is the Abira hoodie set available?

Yes. The Abira hoodie boubou and hoodie pants set is restocked and available for immediate delivery. Message us for the price.

When is the next drop?

Something soft is on the way. Follow @byadabeke or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you the moment it drops.
